💡 The Science

White & Pink Noise

Research shows continuous noise helps infants fall asleep faster by masking environmental disturbances. Pink noise may enhance slow-wave sleep.

Womb Simulation

The womb is surprisingly loud (~80-90 dB). Familiar sounds like heartbeat and blood flow provide comfort through recognition.

Natural Rhythms

Predictable, rhythmic sounds like waves help regulate breathing and heart rate, promoting relaxation and sleep onset.
